# Island art refresh — 31 October 2026

The island now uses richly shaded pixel art that matches the playable visitor and guinea pigs: layered foliage, pink blossoms, bright meadow grass, golden paths, mossy rocks, warm wooden buildings, textured log seats and a stone campfire. The 1280 × 1024 layout and activity positions are preserved. Fourteen additional peripheral trees add depth without narrowing the main paths.

## Assets and generation

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, using the supplied concept as a style reference. New files are stored in the repository, leaving the original assets intact:

- `src/client/assets/sprites/sanctuary-scenery-v2.png` — trees, flowers, rocks, logs, fire, buildings, construction stages, bridge and carrot.
- `src/client/assets/sprites/sanctuary-ground-v2.png` — grass, sand path, water and cliff material quadrants.
- `src/client/assets/sprites/sanctuary-garden-v2.png` — one empty garden bed, five crops and a seedling.

`MeadowArt.ts` trims transparent gutters per object, preserves aspect ratios, and bakes the source regions into small nearest-filtered textures. Full source atlas textures are then released from Phaser. Ground materials are composed into cached 257-pixel chunks with shared edge coverage. No single image is stretched across the world.

## Garden correction

The first integration exposed a layered legacy farm rectangle over a generic construction foundation. Both were removed. There is now one named `farm-bed:0` image on the same shared plot, and crop sprites are shown or hidden as planting, growth and harvest progress. The DOM target remains 110 × 76 pixels, matching the garden's display size. The other wooden platforms are unfinished construction sites; they do not plant crops or act as additional gardens.

## Solid objects

Rendering does not define physics. Shared authoritative feet-level rectangles in `src/shared/meadow.ts` remain the source of collision for tree trunks, boulders, house footprints, fire and wooden logs. New peripheral trees use this same list. Decorative canopy and flower overhangs do not turn into invisible walls. Players slide around solid objects; animals use the same rectangles with additional clearance. Seating can enter a log through the existing validated seat action; movement releases the visitor onto clear ground before walking resumes.

The reference's river/waterfall composition is not recreated in this layout.
